What type of rod system?
Paris Texas Hardware makes both stationary decorative poles and full traverse systems. Choose what fits your project.

Stationary / Decorative Pole
Drapery hangs from rings and slides manually. Rod stays fixed. Best for panels that rarely open and close.

Baton Draw Metal Traverse
Panel glides on a concealed metal track. Operated by baton (wand). Modern, clean look. Available in 1⅛" and 1⅜" diameters. 1⅜" also available in QS Wood & Resin finishes.
1⅛" · 1⅜" · 4' min · up to 20' track · Portfolio, QS Metal & QS Wood (1⅜" only)

R-TEC Motorized Metal Traverse

Choose a Finial Style
Select the finial style collection. Finials are sold in pairs. Prices shown are estimated MSRP from the June 2025 suggested retail price list — final pricing confirmed at quote with current tariff surcharge.
Cuff / Finial Adapter required for traverse: The selected finial style requires a Transitional Cuff (PO138CUFF / QS138CUFF) to attach to the metal traverse track. This will be included in your quote. Without the cuff, finials cannot attach to the traverse rod.
Cuff / Finial Adapter required: All 1⅛" finials require either a Transitional Cuff (PO118CUFF) or Deco Cuff (PO118DECUFF) to attach to 1⅛" metal traverse tracks. This will be noted in your quote.
