Header / carrier type
Pleated carriers: Standard pinch-pleat, euro pleat, goblet pleat. 16mm ball-bearing wheeled carriers. Smooth operation for most drapery weights.
Ripplefold™ carriers: Wave/ripple-fold drapery. Snap-tape or wheeled Butt Master carriers. Specify fullness: 60%, 80%, 100%, or 120%.
Ripplefold™ fullness
60% minimal wave · 80% moderate wave · 100% standard (recommended) · 120% full lush wave.
Rod is ordered with either slides (for pleated) or Ripplefold™ carriers — specify when ordering. Both types use 16mm ball-bearing wheels for smooth, quiet operation.
5
Draw type
Two-way / split draw: Two panels meet in the center and open to both sides. Most common for windows and doorways.
One-way right: Single panel travels entirely to the right side. Good for corner windows or one-sided stack.
One-way left: Single panel travels entirely to the left side.
AMP™ motor is compatible with both one-way and two-way draw. Draw direction must match motor adapter (Right or Left motorhead).
6
Mount type
Wall mount: Standard. Brackets attach to wall above window. Adjustable projection 3¼"–4¾".
Ceiling mount: Track mounts to ceiling. Flat ceiling bracket included. Good for floor-to-ceiling or room dividers.
Double rod: Two rods at different projections — sheer behind, drapery in front. Requires double bracket.

AMP™ motorization
Manual operation: Cord draw, baton draw, or hand-traverse. No motor needed.
AMP™ motorized: Rechargeable battery + wall adapter. RF remote, wall switch, or smart home. Soft start/stop, tug-to-open.
AMP™ Motor specs: DC 16.5V · 2.55Ah · 7.8 in/sec · 1.2Nm torque · UL/CE certified · White only · 6ft power cord (non-extendable — plan outlet location) · Dual battery + AC (works during power outage)

Critical rules: One motor per rod only · Max 64 lbs on motorized rod · Cannot bend or curve the track when motorized · Max track length 16 ft (same as manual)
