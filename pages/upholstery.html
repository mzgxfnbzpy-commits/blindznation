Services
Three types. One expert team.
Whether it’s a 30-panel wall installation, a custom headboard, or a set of throw pillows that need to match your drapes exactly — we design, fabricate, and deliver with the same care every time.
Type 1
Decorative Wall Upholstery
Fabric-wrapped panels installed directly to walls or as floating decorative elements. Perfect for closets, bedrooms, foyers, corridors, and feature walls where you want texture, pattern, and luxury that paint cannot deliver.
  • Any fabric — designer prints, solids, velvets, linens, textured weaves
  • Customer-supplied or we source from our fabric library
  • Flush or paneled installations, any room shape
  • Custom framing, trim, and edge finishing
  • Works behind shelving, cabinetry, and built-ins
Type 2
Acoustic Wall Upholstery
Fabric-wrapped acoustic panels that control echo, flutter, and sound transmission — while looking completely custom. Ideal for home theaters, media rooms, recording spaces, offices, and commercial environments where both performance and appearance matter.
  • Acoustic-grade substrate beneath the fabric for real sound absorption
  • Home theaters, media rooms & recording spaces
  • Commercial & hospitality acoustic applications
  • Any decorative fabric over acoustic core
  • Integrated with room design — panels, dados, column wraps
Type 3
Pillows, Cushions & Furniture
Custom pillows, seat cushions, headboards, ottomans, benches, dining chairs, and full furniture re-upholstery — made to match your fabric, your room, and your style. We fabricate everything in-house so patterns line up and every seam is intentional.
  • Custom throw pillows & bolsters — any size, any fabric
  • Seat cushions, banquettes & bench upholstery
  • Upholstered headboards & footboards
  • Ottoman & storage bench fabrication
  • Dining chair & accent chair re-upholstery
  • Fabric matched to your drapes, roman shades, or wall panels
