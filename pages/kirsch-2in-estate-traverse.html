4
Drapery heading style
Pleated: classic pinch pleat · slip-in or pin-on hooks · 4 carriers/ft standard · heading above or below track. Ripplefold™: no hooks or pins · snaps onto wheeled carriers · less stack-back · identical interior & exterior appearance.
Ripplefold™ fullness
120%: most fabric · fullest look. 100%: standard fullness. 80%: lighter look. 60%: minimal fullness.
Carrier item numbers: 120% = 82120 · 100% = 82100 · 80% = 82080 · 60% = 82060 (all White/Black). Two Sides Snap Pendant #82001 included for each carrier.
Both heading styles work with hand, cord, baton, duplex, and AMP™ motorized draw. Ripplefold™ carriers are wheeled (82xxx series). Pleated uses ball bearing carriers (86700) or buttmaster carriers (84110–84214).
