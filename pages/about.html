We are a small family business, and we are proud of it. Michael J. Healy Installations LLC is built on six decades of hands-on experience, an obsession with getting every detail right, and a genuine love for the craft.

Our story
One family. One standard. No shortcuts.

Michael Healy — father and the backbone of everything we do — has spent over 60 years in the window treatment trade. He learned every stitch, measurement, and mechanism long before most of today's products existed. That depth of knowledge sets the standard for every job that leaves our hands.

His son Justin runs the business day to day — handling every call, measurement, quote, and installation personally. His daughter Sarah brings a designer's eye to custom fabrication, helping clients choose the right fabric, pleat style, and finish to make their space exactly what they imagined.

Together, we've done it all. Floor-to-ceiling motorized drapery. Fabric tent ceilings. Acoustic wall panels. Arch-top custom treatments. Historic homes where nothing is square. There is no job we haven't figured out — and we bring that depth of experience to every project, no matter the size.

We are particular about our work. We put real care into every seam, every bracket, every hem. That is not a marketing line — it is why clients keep calling us back.
