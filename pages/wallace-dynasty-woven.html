About the Dynasty Woven Collection

The Dynasty Woven Collection by Wallace offers 48 woven natural fiber patterns spanning price groups 1 through 5. Every pattern is available as a hobbled roman shade and with edge binding, making this collection ideal for customers who want a structured, tailored look in natural materials.

Patterns include bamboo weaves (B-prefix), bamboo-jute blends (BJ-prefix), and pure jute constructions (J-prefix), ranging from crisp whites and pale neutrals to deeper charcoals and warm earth tones.

  • 48 patterns • Price Groups 1–5
  • Materials: Bamboo, jute, bamboo-jute blends
  • Hobbled shade style available on all patterns
  • Edge binding available on all patterns
  • Styles: Waterfall, Flat Fold, Hobbled — all available
