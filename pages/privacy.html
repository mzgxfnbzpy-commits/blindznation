Privacy Policy

Michael J. Healy Installations LLC — doing business as Blindznation

Overview

Blindznation (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is operated by Michael J. Healy Installations LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you submit a quote request or contact form:

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, and email address
  • Project details: window measurements, product preferences, delivery address for shipping
  • Notes: any additional information you include in the notes field

We also collect basic usage data through Google Analytics (see below).

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request or inquiry
  • To provide pricing, schedule a consultation, or confirm an order
  • To follow up on a submitted quote if we have not heard back
  • To send order confirmations and installation scheduling information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & Analytics

This site does not currently use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics service. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or retargeting cookies. The only browser storage used by this site is a temporary quote cart saved in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

Data retention

Quote request information submitted through our forms is retained in our email inbox. We keep records for as long as they are reasonably needed to manage your project and for standard business record-keeping purposes.

Your rights (California residents — CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Request the categories of personal information we have collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Quote requests submitted on this site open your email client and are sent directly from your device to our inbox — no form data is transmitted through our server. We do not store payment card information on this site. When payment processing is introduced, it will be handled by a PCI-compliant processor (such as Stripe); card data will never touch our servers.

Third-party links

This site may link to product catalogs, manufacturer websites, or booking tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites.
