How it operates

Choose your operating system

Every Soluna can be ordered with any of four operating systems. The right choice depends on your window size, how often you operate the shade, and whether you want smart-home integration.

Cordless Push-Lift
Child & pet safe

Raise and lower with a gentle push or pull on the bottom rail. No cords — clean look, safe for households with children or pets.

  • Max width 72″
  • Max height 84″
  • Cord free Yes
  • Smart home No
Continuous Loop
Most popular

A side-mounted bead chain operates the shade in both directions. Smooth, consistent force regardless of shade weight. Ideal for most windows.

  • Max width 144″
  • Max height 132″
  • Chain material Steel or nylon
  • Smart home No
AutoLift™
Pull-to-operate

Norman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. Pull down to lower, pull again to raise. Best for smaller windows with infrequent operation.

  • Max width 72″
  • Max height 96″
  • Cord free Yes
  • Smart home No
Motorized
Smart home ready

Battery or hardwired motor inside the roller tube. Control by app, remote, wall switch, voice (Alexa/Google/HomeKit), or schedule. Completely cord-free.

  • Max width 144″
  • Max height 132″
  • Motor brands Norman Smart (recommended) · Rollease Acmeda Automate
  • Smart home Yes
Light control options

Four levels of opacity

Every Soluna fabric is organized into one of four light control levels. Within each level there are multiple fabric collections with different textures, weaves, and colorways — current options vary by season.

1% – 14% openness
Solar / Screen

Woven mesh fabric. Reduces glare and UV while maintaining your view. You see out — people cannot easily see in during daylight hours.

Openness: 1% · 3% · 5% · 10% (residential) · 14% (commercial NA series)
Light Filtering

Diffuses sunlight into a soft glow while maintaining privacy during the day. The warmest, most decorative option. Best for living areas and dining rooms.

Multiple fabric collections — call for current options
Room Darkening

Blocks approximately 98% of light. Small amount of light may pass at edges. Ideal for bedrooms, media rooms, and shift workers.

Multiple fabric collections — call for current options
Blackout

100% light block through the fabric. Paired with side channels or a cassette system to seal edges, achieving true total darkness. Nurseries, home theaters, night-shift sleepers.

Blackout fabric + optional side channels / cassette

Installation

Inside or outside mount

Every Soluna can be installed either way. Your window's depth and trim profile determines the best option — we assess this during your free in-home measure.

Inside Mount

The shade fits within the window opening. Clean, recessed look that shows off the window trim. Most popular choice for windows with depth.

Minimum 2¾″ window depth required for headrail. Deductions applied at order.
Outside Mount

The shade is mounted on the wall or trim above the window opening. The shade hangs wider and taller than the opening, maximizing light blockage and making windows appear larger.

Recommended: extend 2–3″ beyond opening on each side and 3–4″ above.
Options & upgrades

Every Soluna is fully custom

Beyond fabric and operating system, every shade can be configured with these additional options.

⬜
Cassette Headrail
Encloses the roller tube in a neat square or round cassette. Plain or fabric-wrapped to match your shade. Standard on most orders.
🪟
Fascia / Valance
Norman Soluna fascia options: Square Fascia (metal), Curved Fascia with Fabric wrap, or Fabric Valance. Conceals the headrail for a clean, finished look.
🔲
Side Channels
Aluminum channels at each side edge guide the shade and seal out light. Required for true blackout performance. Also improves thermal performance.
🔺
Specialty Shapes
Arched, triangular, trapezoidal, and angled windows can be fitted. Pattern cut to the exact shape of your opening. Quote required.
⚡
Motorization Upgrade
Add a motor to any Soluna. Battery motors require no wiring. Hardwired motors available for new construction or renovation. Norman Smart is our default recommendation; Rollease Acmeda Automate available for existing Rollease systems.
🔗
2 or 3 Shades on 1 Headrail
Multiple shades sharing one mounting point on a wide window. Ideal for bay windows or wide openings where a seam in the fabric is acceptable.
