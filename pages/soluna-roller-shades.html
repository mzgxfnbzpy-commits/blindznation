How it operates

Choose your operating system

Every Soluna can be ordered with any of four operating systems. The right choice depends on your window size, how often you operate the shade, and whether you want smart-home integration.

Cordless Push-Lift
Child & pet safe

Raise and lower with a gentle push or pull on the bottom rail. No cords — clean look, safe for households with children or pets.

  • MaNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. width 72″
  • MaNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. height 84″
  • Cord free Yes
  • Smart home No
Continuous Loop
Most popular

A side-mounted bead chain operates the shade in both directions. Smooth, consistent force regardless of shade weight. Ideal for most windows.

  • MaNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. width 144″
  • MaNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. height 132″
  • Chain material Steel or nylon
  • Smart home No
AutoLift™
Pull-to-operate

Norman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. Pull down to lower, pull again to raise. Best for smaller windows with infrequent operation.

  • MaNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. width 72″
  • MaNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system. height 96″
  • Cord free Yes
  • Smart home No

Installation

Inside or outside mount

Every Soluna can be installed either way. Your window's depth and trim profile determines the best option — we assess this during your free in-home measure.

Inside Mount

The shade fits within the window opening. Clean, recessed look that shows off the window trim. Most popular choice for windows with depth.

Minimum 2¾″ window depth required for headrail. Deductions applied at order.
Outside Mount

The shade is mounted on the wall or trim above the window opening. The shade hangs wider and taller than the opening, maNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system.imizing light blockage and making windows appear larger.

Recommended: eNorman's spring-tension AutoLift™ system.tend 2–3″ beyond opening on each side and 3–4″ above.
Options & upgrades

Every Soluna is fully custom

Beyond fabric and operating system, every shade can be configured with these additional options.

⬜
Cassette Headrail
Encloses the roller tube in a neat square or round cassette. Plain or fabric-wrapped to match your shade. Standard on most orders.

Fascia / Valance Board
Norman Soluna fascia options: Square Fascia (metal), Curved Fascia with Fabric wrap, or Fabric Valance. Conceals the headrail for a clean, finished look.

Side Channels
Aluminum channels at each side edge guide the shade and seal out light. Required for true blackout performance. Also improves thermal performance.
