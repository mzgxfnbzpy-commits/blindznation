What type of system?
Traverse Rod — cord-draw system, panels open & close on a track. Lockseam Curtain Rod — stationary rod for rod pocket, grommet, or tab panels. Tension Rod — spring-loaded, no holes needed (café curtains & sheers). Sash Rod — flat or round for door panels, café tiers, small windows.
