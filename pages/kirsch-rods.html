Pole type & length
1" poles: Fixed lengths only — 4' (48"), 6' (72"), 8' (96"). No telescoping for 1". Poles join with internal splice #1042812 (Bright Zinc) for spans beyond 8'.
1⅜" poles: Fixed 4'/6'/8' or Telescoping 36"–66", 66"–120", 120"–180". Telescoping cuts to any exact length within its range. Internal splice #73304061 for spans over 180". All 8 finishes available in both fixed and telescoping.
Bracket type
1" brackets are sold per finish to match your pole. Weight limits are per bracket. Add a center bracket for poles over 48". All 8 finishes.
Bypass: Two rods pass at different depths from one bracket point — for rooms needing two panel layers that overlap at center. French Return: Panel wraps around the side of the rod so the end of the fabric touches the wall (no visible bracket gap).
1⅜" brackets sold per finish to match pole. Weight limits are per bracket. Add center bracket for spans over 48". All 8 finishes.
Inside mount Socket (#1832218): Rod rests inside casing — no wall holes. Requires ≈1½" casing depth minimum. Swivel Socket (#1831428): Allows rod to angle for bay or angled frames. Double Rod bracket: Both a front and back pole mount from one wall bracket — inner 3"–4" projection, outer 6"–7". Baton with Bypass C-Ring (#1831558, 36"): Mention in notes if needed.
Ring type
1" rings: int 1⅜", ext 1-11/16". Sold per finish. Lock Ring is for French return only.
1⅜" rings: int 1¾", ext 2⅛". Sold per finish. Lock Ring is for French return only.
