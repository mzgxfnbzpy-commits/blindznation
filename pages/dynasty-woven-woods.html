Shade style
—
Waterfall — 4″ back valance, no front valance, rolls up in soft folds. Flat Fold — 6″ front valance, crisp horizontal folds. Hobbled — 6″ front valance, continuous soft folds when raised · 30% surcharge.
4
Lift & control
—
Loop Chain — stainless or bronze metal chain, 1.5″ slim or 2.5″ regular clutch headrail, Waterfall & Flat Fold only. Cordless (certified) — child & pet safe, Waterfall & Flat Fold only, 1.5″ headrail. Cordless TDBU — top-down bottom-up, Flat Fold only, max 72″ × 72″, pattern restrictions apply. Motorized (certified) — Rollease Acmeda Automate motor, rechargeable Li-ion, Waterfall & Flat Fold only, min 26″ × 36″. Dual Shade — front woven + back liner, 3.5″ headrail, Waterfall & Flat Fold only, requires Privacy or Blackout liner.
Motorization accessories
Select any. Automate™ Li-ion motor (25mm/1.1Nm) is included when Motorized is selected. Paradigm Remote — RF 15-channel. Pulse 2 Hub ships separately. Charger quantity ordered separately per shade.
5
Liner
No liner
No liner — natural woven only. Privacy liner — light filtering, exterior side off-white. Blackout liner — exterior side off-white.
Liner color
If liner is added to a Hobbled shade, the liner surcharge is also subject to the 30% hobbled surcharge. Liner will have a horizontal seam for clean folding on Dual Shade. BO liner code suffix: P = Privacy, BO = Blackout.
6
Edge binding
No edge binding
Twill sewn on all edges. ½″ Twill — color-coordinated thread, sewn L/R/bottom + L/R valance. 1½″ Twill — mitered corners, color-coordinated thread.
7
Valance
Standard valance
Standard valance — included with shade. Hobble valance replaces the standard valance for a textured layered look: 12″ Double Hobble (2 folds) or 18″ Triple Hobble (3 folds). 38mm wood headrail. Each fold is 6″.
