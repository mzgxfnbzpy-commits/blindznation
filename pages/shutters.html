2 Shutter line
The line sets the colors, louver sizes and max panel widths. Normandy® — hardwood (paint) or Phoenix wood (stain); widest color range (24 paint, 18 stain, 6 OSMO); panels up to 42″. Woodlore® — composite wood with a polypropylene coating; resists moisture, warping and UV; 6 classic white/neutral colors. Woodlore® Plus — composite frame + ABS louvers; 24 colors; optional fully waterproof version with stainless hinges; specialty shapes.
3 Frame situation
Most shutters fit in the window opening. A direct mount frame attaches to the face of the window frame (exact or window-opening size). Tracking & sliding systems are for bypass, bi-fold or barn-door configurations. Minimum depth: 1⅞″ louvers need ~¾″ clearance; 4½″ louvers need ~1¾″. We confirm during measurement.

6 T-posts

T-posts are stationary center dividers. Vertical T-posts split the shutter width. Horizontal T-posts split double-hung panels. Required when your layout includes "T".

Horizontal T-post: 10% surcharge applies. Default type: Chamfer T-post unless otherwise specified. Custom T-post width up to 7½″ available (additional surcharge). Section length per T-post ≤48″.

7 Louver size

Larger louvers mean a clearer view when open and a bolder look. Smaller louvers offer a traditional style.

Choose a shutter line in Step 2 to see its louver sizes.
8 Tilt rod / louver control
⚠ InvisibleTilt™ is not available with 1⅞″ louvers. Please select a larger louver size or choose Visible tilt bar.
InvisibleTilt™ embeds the tilt mechanism inside the stile — no visible rod. Surcharge applies. Not available for 1⅞″ louvers.
9 Frame style

The frame surrounds the shutter panel. Most inside-mount orders use an L Frame. Z frames work for window casings with specific depth. Hang strips are low-profile.

Sill / bottom plate
3-sided frame omits the bottom plate — the window sill acts as the base. Use when you want to preserve the sill appearance or the sill protrudes into the opening.
10 Divider rails

A divider rail adds a horizontal mid-panel rail so louvers can be controlled independently above and below. Required on tall panels.

Split tilt can sometimes eliminate the need for a divider rail. If you select split tilt in Specialty Options, confirm with us before finalizing.
