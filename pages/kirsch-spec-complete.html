Bracket Spacing Rule Universal
Rule: End brackets at both ends. At stack ends (where fabric piles up): first center bracket within 24" of the end bracket, then every 48" intervals from there.
Example — 12 ft two-way split: End | 24" | bracket | 48" | bracket | 48" | bracket | 24" | End = 5 brackets
Reduce spacing for heavy or bulky fabrics. Center brackets are adjustable in projection.

Model Details
94001 — AMP™ Capable MOTORIZABLE
Heavy-duty ball-bearing pulleys. Wall, ceiling, or recessed mount. Can be bent or curved for bay windows (MANUAL ONLY — not motorized). Custom sizes ordered to spec.
Colors: White · Gray · Bronze · Black
Track: 1½" × 1-11/16" cross-section
Bracket: #94270 — adjustable 3¼"–4¾" projection
Manual max: One-Way 20ft/80lbs · Two-Way 40ft/160lbs
AMP max: Both draw types 16ft/64lbs
Pleated + Ripplefold™: Both compatible
94003 — Recommended for Most Jobs RECOMMENDED
Most versatile standard residential track. Wall, ceiling, or recessed mount. Can be curved for bay windows (manual only). No AMP motorization.
Colors: White #94373025 · Gray #94373090 · Bronze #94373064 · Black #94373059
Manual max: One-Way 20ft/80lbs · Two-Way 40ft/160lbs
AMP: Not compatible
Best for: Most residential installs, bay windows, large windows without motorization needs
94004 — Easy Ceiling Mount
Flange on back for flush ceiling installation. Ceiling mount only — cannot wall mount. Manual only, no bending.
Best for: Clean ceiling installations, new construction where track can be hidden above ceiling line
AMP: Not compatible
94005 — NOT RECOMMENDED ⚠
Screws mount directly through track face — no standard brackets. Limited access for adjustment or repair. Cannot bend, no AMP.
Use only when: No other mounting option exists and access is not a concern
Wall + ceiling: Both available
9046 — Commercial / Hospital Grade
Heavy-duty commercial track for hospital cubicle curtains, hotels, large commercial spaces. Ceiling or recessed mount only. Can be bent (8"–12" radius). Manual only, pleated headers only.
One-Way: Max 16ft / 64 lbs
Two-Way: Max 32ft / 128 lbs
Mount: Ceiling · Recessed only
Headers: Pleated only (not Ripplefold)
9600 — Heavy Duty / Extra Strength
Maximum capacity track for very heavy draperies, hotels, theaters, commercial use. All mount types including suspension. Can be bent (12"–14" radius). Manual only, pleated headers only.
One-Way: Max 20ft / 80 lbs
Two-Way: Max 40ft / 160 lbs
Mount: Wall · Ceiling · Recessed · Suspension
K-Rail — Decorative Traverse
Unique product: a functional traverse track that accepts 1⅜" finials from Kirsch decorative collections (Designer Metals, Wood Trends, Wrought Iron). Combines the look of a decorative rod with smooth traverse operation. Wall or ceiling mount. Cannot bend. Manual only.
One-Way + Two-Way: Both max 16ft / 64 lbs
Headers: Pleated AND Ripplefold™ compatible
Compatible finials: All 1⅜" Wrought Iron finials · All 1⅜" Wood Trends finials · Designer Metals 1⅜" finials
Critical Restrictions Summary
✗ AMP motor: 94001 ONLY
No other Architrac model can be motorized. 94003, 94004, 94005, 9046, 9600, K-Rail are all manual only.
✗ No bending when motorized
94001 with AMP cannot be bent or curved. Bending is manual 94001/94003/9046/9600 only.
⚠ One motor per rod
Never install two AMP motors on a single Architrac. One motor only, ever.
⚠ Pleated only for 9046/9600
9046 and 9600 do not support Ripplefold™ carriers — pleated headers only.
✓ K-Rail accepts decorative finials
K-Rail is the only Architrac track compatible with 1⅜" decorative finials from DM/WT/WI collections.
✓ 94001 bend: manual only
94001 can be bent for bay windows or curved applications — but ONLY when installed as manual (no AMP).
